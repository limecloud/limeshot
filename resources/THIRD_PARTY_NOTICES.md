# Third-party Resource Notices

No third-party Skill, prompt, script, template, model catalog, media sample, or provider adapter is included in this resource tree.

The files under `resources/` are LimeShot-authored declarations. The official OpenAI Codex release selected for packaging is declared in `codex/manifest.v1.json` with its release URL, Apache-2.0 license, and archive/executable SHA-256 values. The binary itself is not committed to this resource tree.

Node.js and FFmpeg distributions remain unselected. They must not enter a package until their exact version, source, license, build configuration, integrity hashes, and redistribution notice are approved.
